# Setting up MFA on a new phone

## Symptom
User has replaced their phone and can no longer approve sign-in requests, or
the Authenticator app no longer lists the work account.

## Resolution when the old phone is still available
1. On the old phone, open Microsoft Authenticator, tap the work account, then
   the settings icon.
2. On the new phone, install Microsoft Authenticator and sign in with the work
   account. Approve the prompt on the old phone.
3. Confirm the new phone shows the account and can approve a test sign-in at
   https://myaccount.microsoft.com.

## Resolution when the old phone is gone
The account needs its MFA methods reset by IT before the new phone can be
registered. Verify identity by video call or in person before doing this. Do
not reset MFA methods off the back of an email request alone: MFA reset by
email request is the most common way an account takeover succeeds.

## Scope
All staff. VIP accounts require verification by the requester's line manager
in addition to the above.
